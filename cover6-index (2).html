About the Band

Five Players.
One Sound.

Cover 6 is a five-piece live cover band bringing energy, chops, and serious groove to stages across the Baton Rouge area. From classic rock anthems to the hits you're dancing to right now, we cover five decades of the best music ever made — and we make it feel like ours.

Whether you're booking a festival, a corporate event, a bar night, or a private party, one thing is guaranteed: the room will move. We've got you covered.
