About Jordan

Jordan Tupper is a Baton Rouge-based musician, bassist, vocalist, music educator, and community leader with nearly two decades of experience performing, teaching, and leading in music.

With Cover 6 Band, Jordan handles bass and vocals as part of a five-piece group built for live rooms, private events, festivals, restaurants, and dance floors. His broader music work includes classic rock, funk, soul, contemporary covers, and jazz performance across the Baton Rouge area.

Jordan also performs with Night Hog and Standley, Tupper & Petersen, connecting Cover 6 to a wider network of Baton Rouge live music, Louisiana cover bands, and jazz performance.
