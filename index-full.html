About the Band

Five Players.
One Sound.

Cover 6 is a five-piece live cover band from the Baton Rouge area, playing rock, pop, funk, soul, and crowd favorites from the 70s to today. The band features David Martin on lead vocals, Sam Jeter on lead guitar, Nick Fehn on rhythm guitar, Jordan Tupper on bass and vocals, and Coty on drums.

From classic rock anthems to the hits you're dancing to right now, Cover 6 covers five decades of the best music ever made. Whether you're booking a festival, a corporate event, a bar night, or a private party, one thing is guaranteed: the room will move. We've got you covered.
